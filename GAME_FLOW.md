### **1. Storyline Selection**

- Each player joins the lobby (2–4 players).
- The group selects or is assigned a **storyline/theme** (e.g., “Lost in Space”, “Haunted Manor”, “Cyberpunk Heist”).
- The base AI prompt is initialized with this storyline context.

---

### **2. Story Introduction**

- The AI Game Master generates an **opening paragraph**, setting the stage for the adventure.
- The “What happens next?” page appears for all players.

---

### **3. Player Turn Phase**

- Each player submits a short prompt or action describing **how they want the story to continue**.
- (Optional: A short timer may limit how long players have to write their input.)

---

### **4. Voting Phase**

- All player submissions are revealed **anonymously**.
- Players vote on their favorite continuation (cannot vote for their own).
- The **winning prompt** becomes the next part of the story.
- The player whose prompt was chosen earns **points**.

---

### **5. AI Continuation**

- The AI generates the **next paragraph** of the story, incorporating the winning player’s idea.
- The story evolves dynamically based on player choices and AI interpretation.

---

### **6. Progression and Ending**

- Each new turn repeats the loop:
    - Player input → Voting → AI continuation.
- As the **turn number increases**, the AI prompt is updated with an instruction to **begin wrapping up the story** (e.g., “The adventure nears its conclusion…”).
- After a set number of turns (e.g., 5–8), the AI produces a **final paragraph** concluding the tale.

---

### **7. Scoring and Winner**

- The player with the **most votes (points)** throughout the game is declared the **Story Champion**.
- Optionally, display:
    - The **full story transcript**
    - Player contributions highlighted
    - Voting summary and stats

---

### **Optional Enhancements**

- **AI narrator voiceover** for each paragraph
- **Dynamic artwork or music** that evolves with the story’s tone
- **Difficulty or chaos mode** where AI introduces twists
